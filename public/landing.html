About ILBBEC

Shaping Tomorrow's Global Communicators & Logistics Leaders

ILBBEC is the official student center at Universitas Logistik & Bisnis Internasional (ULBI) dedicated to bridging specialized international logistics terminology, global commerce, and English language excellence.

Global Communication

Master fluent speech, spontaneous discussion, parliamentary debate, and diplomatic negotiation in an engaging 100% English atmosphere.

Logistics & Trade Acumen

Acquire specialized international freight, supply chain terminology, trade contracts, and case-study presentation competence.

Leadership & Networking

Develop organizational management, cross-faculty collaboration, and join international student symposiums and youth forums.

Career & Certifications

Targeted preparation for TOEFL ITP/iBT, IELTS, business portfolio building, and job interview simulations for multinational corporations.

Why Join ILBBEC at ULBI?

A welcoming, supportive student community where beginners build confidence and experienced speakers hone competitive mastery.
